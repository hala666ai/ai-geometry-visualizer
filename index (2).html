How to use this tool in class
  • Ask students to predict how area and volume change when the size doubles.
  • Let them rotate the shape and describe what stays the same and what changes.
  • Use the drawing area to discuss why a circle and rectangle with the same area look different.
  • Challenge them to “beat the AI” by drawing shapes it misclassifies, then discuss why.
